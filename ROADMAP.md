## Next

(224 filters still skipped; **1.5** did `trusted-replace-argument`, `href-sanitizer`, `trusted-prevent-dom-bypass`, the `json-edit` family and `edit-inbound/outbound-object`)
- Performance: the browser's own cost of applying the rules is now most of Shield's overhead; a smaller `.com` page-fix bundle (0.7 MB, loaded in every `.com` frame)
- **Hallow family:** "blocked today" tile in Hallow Remote / Hub (needs a small native bridge - the extension itself has no way to talk to the apps yet)
- Publish: Chrome Web Store / Edge Add-ons / addons.mozilla.org listings (needs your developer accounts)
- Regional lists as opt-in rulesets (EasyList Germany, Liste FR, ...) - there's room: ~23.9k of 30k rules used with every list on
- Zapper: a preview of everything else a selector matches before you hide it (picking inside frames: **done in 1.3**, same-site frames)
- Stats without the optional permission (the browser gives no counts without it), and a "blocked today" tile for the Hallow apps
