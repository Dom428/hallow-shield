My filters

Your own filters, one per line, in Adblock Plus / uBlock Origin syntax. They stay in the browser on this PC and are never sent anywhere.

  • example.com##.newsletter-box hides something on one site; ##.thing on every site (procedural filters like :has-text() work with a site in front).
  • ||ads.example.com^ blocks requests; @@||cdn.example.com/lib.js lets one through; options such as $script, $third-party, $domain=, $important, $removeparam= work as in the lists. They become the browser's own rules when you save.
  • Scriptlet filters (##+js(...)) aren't accepted here: Shield only runs the page fixes that ship with it.
  • What you hide with Hide something on this page in the popup is saved here as site##selector.

          Honest limits
          • The browser decides what an extension may know. Blocked counts come from the browser's list of the rules it applied (read about once a minute and when a tab closes, within its limit of 20 reads per 10 minutes); pages you leave quickly may be under-counted.
          • The blocked host is only known when the rule that blocked it names one host. Many hosts share one rule (that's how Shield fits the lists into the browser's limits); those are counted as "shared host rules".
          • The site is the tab's site at the time; requests from service workers or closed tabs count without a site.
          • "Ads" are the uBlock filters, EasyList and Peter Lowe's lists; "trackers" EasyPrivacy and uBlock Privacy; the rest is "other". A request two lists would block is counted once, for the list the browser used.
          • Cookie-banner clicks are counted by Shield itself, one per "Reject" (or "settings", then "Reject") click.
