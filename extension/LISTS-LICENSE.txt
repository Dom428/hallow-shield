filter lists. The lists are data with their own licenses; Hallow Shield's code is its own.

      https://easylist.to/   by The EasyList authors and Fanboy (the annoyance lists as split by kind and mirrored in

      https://pgl.yoyo.org/adservers/   by Peter Lowe
      License: McRae General Public License (version 4.r53) - see the list's own page; free to use and redistribute
      with credit.

The converted files are shared under the same terms as the lists they come from (for the EasyList-derived parts:
Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0). They were changed: converted to the browser's declarativeNetRequest
format, CSS, JSON programs for procedural filters and arguments for Shield's own scriptlets; filters the browser
can't use were left out. rules/meta.json names the list versions used and what was left out.

The approach (static rulesets, merged host rules, scriptlets registered per site in the page's world) follows
uBlock Origin Lite (https://github.com/uBlockOrigin/uBOL-home, GPLv3). No uBlock Origin or uBlock Origin Lite code is
included: the converter, the scriptlets, the procedural filter engine and the redirect stand-ins in web/ are Hallow
Shield's own.
